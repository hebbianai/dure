# Dure community Code of Conduct

We want people of all backgrounds and experience levels to be able to contribute
to Dure. This policy applies to this project's issues, pull requests, reviews and
other community spaces managed by Hebbian AI.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate. Critique the work and explain your reasoning.
- Welcome questions, accept constructive feedback and respect others' time.
- Respect privacy, consent and differences in background or experience.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and help resolve disagreements calmly.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, personal attacks,
deliberate disruption and publishing another person's private information
without permission are not acceptable.

## Moderation and reporting

Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock discussions, and restrict
participation when needed to protect the community. Their response should be
proportionate to the behavior, its impact and any repeated violations. These
expectations also apply to maintainers.

For abusive content on GitHub, use the content's **Report content** menu or
[GitHub's abuse report form](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse).
GitHub handles reports submitted through that form. Avoid posting private
details in a public discussion when seeking help.

For product security vulnerabilities, use the separate private reporting route
in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
