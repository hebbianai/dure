# Dure name, branding and official builds

Hebbian AI uses the Dure name, logo and app icons to identify the Dure project
and its official distributions. This policy covers their use as project and
product branding. It does not change the [MIT license](LICENSE), revoke existing
copyright permissions, or restrict the rights to use, modify, build, sign,
redistribute or sell the software under its applicable licenses. Third-party
names and marks belong to their respective owners.

## Referring to Dure

You may use the Dure name to truthfully describe the project, link to it, discuss
it in documentation or reviews, and explain compatibility or the origin of a
fork. Screenshots and the unmodified logo may identify Dure in those contexts.
Keep the reference accurate and avoid suggesting that Hebbian AI sponsors,
endorses or publishes your work.

## Forks and community builds

You may build Dure for personal use and contribute changes without replacing
the branding in your development checkout.

When distributing a modified product, use a distinct product name, logo and app
icon so users can identify its publisher. You may describe it as "based on Dure"
and must retain the copyright and license notices required by the source
licenses. Those notices are attribution, not product branding.

Clearly label independently compiled distributions as community or unofficial
builds and identify their publisher, even when the source is unchanged. You may
redistribute an unmodified official build with accurate attribution to its
original publisher; mirroring it does not make you an official Dure publisher.

## Official builds

Official Dure builds are published by Hebbian AI through the download and
release links on [dureai.dev](https://www.dureai.dev/). Publisher signing,
notarization and update delivery belong to that distribution process. A local
build or a build signed by another publisher is not an official Hebbian AI build.

The public source includes desktop and mobile projects. Availability of an
official build for a particular platform is separate from the presence of its
source; see the [current platform coverage](CONTRIBUTING.md#platforms).

## Other branding uses

For permission to use Dure branding as the identity of your own product or
service, contact the maintainers through the project's
[community channels](README.md#contributing). This policy does not limit uses
otherwise permitted by applicable law.
